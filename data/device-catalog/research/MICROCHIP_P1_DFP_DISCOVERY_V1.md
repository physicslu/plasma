# Microchip P1 Phase 1 — exact commercial ICPN discovery expansion

## Owner priority

Increase the Microchip P1 Research ICPN population before individual
Phase 2 software support, programming interface, geometry, electrical
conditions, programming routines, and HIL investigations.

## P1 tiers and strict meanings

P1_TARGET_CFG: **126 frozen** original-MCP exact commercial partNumber
to source-exact manufacturer Base and original DFP mapping to pinned
OpenOCD Target-only cfg (atsaml1x.cfg). NOT software supported.

P1_DFP_DISCOVERY: new original-MCP exact commercial partNumber /
basePartNumber to original manufacturer DFP Support Devices *exact Base*
name for qualified discovery-lane 32bit-pic-sam, with no already-indexed
OpenOCD Target CFG. This is a *new Phase 1 discovery priority for further
OpenOCD CFG reverse coverage*, **not a P1 Target-CFG qualification**.

The combined P1 Research total is an aggregation of **non-equivalent
evidence tiers**. The source-locked 126 Target-only count and P0 2,437
remain unchanged. No claim of all Microchip ICPNs or device applicability.

## Evidence and denominator

- All 23,276 original-source-replayed exact commercial MCP part numbers,
  with original response SHA and manufacturer-provided basePartNumber.
- The original publisher Microchip DFP pack index source, SHA-256
  9a00c71e262882687219fce322169435a36d45ac06d0a26bf146c4d6a5db83b0.
- Only non-overlapping original-MCP records previously classified as
  NO_DFP_NAME_CFG_CROSSWALK with manufacturer Base matching a unique
  unrestricted original DFP 32bit PIC/SAM support name are added to
  P1_DFP_DISCOVERY.
- Source-pack name, version, DFP original SHA, and original MCP exact-SKU
  source SHA refs are retained per new record.
- 8-bit, 16-bit, MPU, wireless, accessory, unclassified, source ambiguity,
  Base-name mismatch, and all preexisting P0/P1 are excluded from this
  expansion. Other excluded records remain historical Research P2,
  not automatically OpenOCD unsupported.
- Real P1 Discovery counts are dynamically generated by source replay.
  Synthetic tests do not establish a new candidate denominator.

## Boundaries

No Phase 1 exact-field new VERIFIED promotion, internal identity
admission, software SUPPORTED, Flash Driver declaration, Phase 2
Programming Profile, Production Catalog, public IC Selector, PPU or HIL
qualification. Research-only GitHub CI output requires review.
Missing OpenOCD CFG in the bounded 0.12 indexed set is not a proof of
OpenOCD or alternative backend non-support.

Next: review highest-population manufacturer DFP packs and then expand
source-locked OpenOCD CFG coverage where justified. Do not artificially
inflate source-bound counts or silently rename DFP-only research as Target
CFG support.
