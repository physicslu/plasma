## Capability-unresolved identities remain explicit

The two manufacturer-valid Active N-product-version identities are **not** published:

- `STM32G0B1CBT6N`
- `STM32G0B1CBU6N`

Their retained state remains:

- identity: `manufacturer_verified_active`
- capability: `openocd_ordering_pattern_unresolved`
- action: `exclude_from_canonical_admission_until_positive_capability_evidence`

This is not identity rejection. Phase 4.8E does not rewrite them to non-N siblings and does not infer N/non-N programming capability equivalence.

The publication audit permanently records both identities, their capability status, and the reason they remain outside canonical/Production. This prevents later coverage accounting from confusing "not published" with "not a valid manufacturer identity".

## Explicit non-claims

Phase 4.8E publishes a catalog decision. It does not prove that every STM32G0 device is physically qualified by Plasma, and it does not prove that the unresolved N variants use the same programming behavior as their non-N siblings.

The truthful Production statement after closure is therefore:

> STM32G0 has 47 published exact ICPNs across 12 Base Devices. Two additional manufacturer-valid Active exact N-product-version identities are retained outside Production pending positive programming-capability evidence.
